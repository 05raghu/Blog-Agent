# Codewave editorial image prompt system

Use these as structured starting points. Replace bracketed text with the real argument; do not generate visual variety before the editorial concept is clear.

## Architecture of Observation

Use one documented Indian architectural structure as an intellectual instrument, not as decorative heritage.

1. Choose one root: Konark for solar cycles, Jantar Mantar for measurement, Rani-ki-Vav for recursive depth, or Dholavira for systems and flow.
2. State the business or human argument before naming the monument.
3. Build a high-contrast editorial photomontage with flat color, hard cut-paper geometry and raw halftone grain.
4. Let several readings, routes, shadows or levels coexist.
5. Use Signal Cyan `#A8DDE8` only where one reading becomes observed, selected or connected.
6. Use one dominant field and at most one interruption color.
7. Name the output as a contemporary expression informed by the architecture, never as an exact historical reconstruction.

Avoid deity collage, generic mandalas, chakra diagrams, tourism-postcard framing, glossy 3D, neon circuits, gold effects, saffron shorthand and embedded text.

## Collapse — default brand image

Create a premium editorial photomontage about **[one consequential human choice]**. Show **[one recognisable person or gesture]** occupying **[two or three mutually incompatible outcomes]** within the same flat composition. On the resolved version, make exactly one outcome crisp and frame or mark it with Signal Cyan `#A8DDE8`; let the other outcomes remain imperfect monochrome print ghosts. Use high-contrast black-and-white photography, hard cut-paper edges, raw halftone grain, slightly imperfect ink registration and brutalist flatness. Dominant field: Parchment `#EDE7DA`; subject: Ink `#0A0A0B`; one Oxidised Copper `#B3733E` interruption. Intelligent, dry, human and editorial.

## Entangle — campaign and transformation image

Create a surreal editorial photomontage connecting **[a small human action]** to **[a distant, disproportionate consequence]**. Place the two at visibly different scales or in incompatible contexts. In the unresolved version, the relationship is absent. In the resolved version, draw exactly one impossible Signal Cyan `#A8DDE8` thread, path or shared contour that makes the causality legible. Use a Vivid Azure `#438FE7` dominant field, Ink `#0A0A0B` photographic cutouts and a single Ember Orange `#E66D3A` interruption. Raw halftone, hard collage edges, flat print, intelligent absurdity.

## Interfere — editorial and culture image

Create an editorial photomontage about **[conflicting actions, interpretations or futures]**. Repeat or overlap **[one human subject and their gesture]** as interference bands so the scene is understandable but unresolved. In the resolved version, one local circular observation aperture reveals **[the meaningful action]** in Signal Cyan `#A8DDE8` without clearing the rest of the image. Use an Iris Violet `#6552C7` field, Ink `#0A0A0B` photographic cutouts and one Sulphur Yellow `#F2EC67` counterpoint. Raw halftone grain, imperfect screen-print registration, hard flat shapes and restrained surreal humor.

## Universal negative prompt

No GPU, semiconductor, chip, circuit board, server rack, glowing node network, cable, generic AI brain, data tunnel, hologram, sci-fi cube, polished 3D render, glassmorphism, neon gradient, corporate stock-photo polish, illegible body anatomy, blue human skin, embedded typography, logo or watermark.

## Production record

Store with every final image:

- editorial argument;
- chosen behavior;
- source or generation prompt;
- source-image license or consent;
- master dimensions and crop-safe variants;
- alternative text;
- unresolved and observed files where interaction is used.
