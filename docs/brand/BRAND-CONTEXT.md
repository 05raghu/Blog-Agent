# Codewave Quantum Editorial Brand System

## Brand thesis

**Everything is possible until a choice is made.**

Codewave helps people make consequential technology choices visible, testable and real. The brand therefore shows possibility as a state—not as futuristic decoration—and makes interaction the act that resolves it.

The system combines a classical editorial voice with a bold, human visual argument. It must feel intelligent without becoming clinical, surprising without becoming random, and technological without depicting hardware theatre.

## The three quantum behaviors

### Collapse — the master language

Use for roughly 60% of branded expression: corporate pages, core capability narratives, interface moments, case-study systems and high-trust communication.

- Show one human situation with two or more plausible outcomes.
- Use Signal Cyan to identify the observed or selected state.
- Keep typography and composition restrained.
- Interaction resolves multiple possibilities into one crisp outcome.

### Entangle — the story language

Use for roughly 25%: campaigns, partnerships, QuantumAgile stories, transformation narratives and social media.

- Connect a small decision to a distant, disproportionate consequence.
- Pair two scales, contexts or moments that would not normally share a frame.
- Let one Signal Cyan relation reveal itself on interaction.
- Favor Vivid Azure with Ember as the interruption.

### Interfere — the expression language

Use for roughly 15%: editorial covers, reports, culture, launches and rare high-impact digital moments.

- Overlap incompatible actions or perspectives as interference bands.
- Let clarity exist inside a local observation aperture.
- Favor Iris with Sulphur as the counterpoint.
- Do not use repeatedly down a single page.

### Presenting all three behaviors together

Use the **Expanding Quantum Triptych** when Collapse, Entangle and Interfere must appear as one system:

- Default state: Collapse `6fr`, Entangle `3fr`, Interfere `3fr`.
- Entangle engaged: Collapse `3fr`, Entangle `6fr`, Interfere `3fr`.
- Interfere engaged: Collapse `3fr`, Entangle `3fr`, Interfere `6fr`.
- Hover, keyboard focus or tap transfers both visual width and semantic motion to the engaged behavior.
- The exchange uses the 1450 ms Signature Layout token with a symmetric `cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)` curve; do not substitute a fast ease-out.
- Collapse resolves possible states; Entangle draws the distant connection; Interfere opens a movable local aperture.
- Only the active panel exposes supporting copy. The inactive panels retain their title, allocation and unresolved state.
- Below tablet width, stack the panels and preserve tap/focus state changes without forcing horizontal motion.

## Non-negotiable visual rules

1. Human choices before technology objects.
2. Flat print before polished 3D.
3. Signal Cyan always means observed, selected, connected or authored.
4. One dominant field, one subject ink, Signal Cyan and at most one interruption color.
5. Halftone and grain add evidence of making; they never reduce faces or key product information to noise.
6. Quantum behavior is a narrative mechanism, not clip art.
7. Never use chips, server racks, glowing nodes, data tunnels, neon cables, sci-fi cubes or generic AI brains.

## Color governance

- Interface recipe: Ink + soft paper + Signal Cyan.
- Editorial recipe: one dominant field + Ink/Parchment + Signal Cyan + one secondary interruption.
- Copper is material and human; Azure is expansive; Ember is catalytic; Iris is speculative; Sulphur creates friction; Pewter grounds.
- Editorial brights never become permanent capability labels or alternate CTA colors.
- Body text uses Ink on light fields or Paper on Ink. Copper, Iris and Ember are never used for small body copy.

## Cultural grammar

Codewave should feel global and unmistakably from India without becoming nostalgic or ceremonial. The expression family is called **Architecture of Observation**. It draws from Indian structures that measured solar cycles, mapped the sky, engineered depth and organized water. India is the instrument, not the ornament.

Each expression preserves one structural truth from a documented place, then adds one Codewave quantum behavior. The result is a contemporary abstraction, never an architectural reconstruction or a heritage motif traced for decoration.

### Approved instruments

| Instrument | Indian root | Structural truth | Codewave behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solar Measure | Konark Sun Temple | Twenty-four monumental wheels express cyclical time | Several readings coexist until one Signal Cyan reading is observed |
| Observational Cut | Jantar Mantar, Jaipur | Fixed masonry instruments read a moving sky | Several relations coexist until one measured line becomes useful |
| Recursive Descent | Rani-ki-Vav, Patan | Seven architectural levels organize depth and procession | Each level removes noise and sharpens intent |
| System Flow | Dholavira | Planned stone systems organize water, movement and storage | Many channels become one legible operating flow |

Primary source records: [Konark Sun Temple](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/246), [Jantar Mantar](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1338/), [Rani-ki-Vav](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/922/) and [Dholavira](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1645/).

### Construction rule

1. Start from one architectural principle, not a generic Indian symbol.
2. Choose one quantum behavior: coexist, connect, interfere or resolve.
3. Use one dominant editorial field, Ink or Parchment, Signal Cyan and at most one counterpoint.
4. At rest, the composition must already be colorful and complete.
5. Hover, focus or tap reveals measurement, relation, depth or flow. It must add information rather than merely recolor the asset.
6. Keep human consequence present in editorial imagery. Architectural diagrams may omit people only when they teach the system clearly.

### Placement rhythm

- Core brand: no architectural instrument unless the content is specifically about observation, systems or Indian provenance.
- Editorial image: one dominant instrument integrated with a human or business argument.
- Signature event or launch: one large instrument may lead the entire composition.
- Brand guideline: four quiet background crops only, one each for Konark, Jantar Mantar, Rani-ki-Vav and Dholavira.
- Social: one dominant instrument only.
- Product interface: rare onboarding, selected-state or empty-state use only.
- Buttons, inputs and ordinary navigation: no architectural motifs. Preserve the clean 45px action capsule.

### Cultural density

- Core brand and product interface: 0 to 5 percent. Use a single calibrated line or no instrument.
- Editorial and case studies: 8 to 15 percent. Use one architectural system or one quiet structural field.
- Campaign and event work: 20 to 35 percent. One approved instrument may lead; the others stay absent.
- Use flat Ink, Parchment, Copper and Signal Cyan. One approved editorial bright may interrupt when the concept requires it.
- Use bilingual or regional-language content only when it is true to the audience, story or evidence. Never use script as texture.
- Temples and other specific Indian references are allowed when their geometry, construction or story is relevant. Sacred imagery and deity imagery require explicit context and review.
- Avoid generic mandalas, chakra diagrams, copied historic ornament, decorative bouquets, pasted lotus or paisley, tourism clichés, gold effects, saffron shorthand and literal monument postcards.
- Do not claim that a contemporary diagram is an exact historical reconstruction. Name it as an expression informed by the documented architecture.

## Typography

- Signifier Regular 400 is the only display voice. Never use bold Signifier.
- Söhne Buch is the reading voice. Söhne Kraftig is limited to controls, short labels and accessibility-critical emphasis.
- Söhne Mono labels evidence, state, index and system metadata.
- Headlines are sentence case, one clear thought, ideally one or two lines.
- Avoid title case, faux technical strings and decorative all-caps display copy.

### Production type roles

| Role | Element | Family / weight | Fluid size | Leading | Tracking | Measure | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | H1 | Signifier Regular 400 | 72–152px | 0.84 | −0.062em | 8–10ch | One page thesis only |
| Section | H2 | Signifier Regular 400 | 52–122px | 0.88 | −0.055em | 8–12ch | One clear thesis per section |
| Editorial | H3 | Signifier Regular 400 | 32–52px | 1.0 | −0.035em | Up to 14ch | Cards and chapter titles |
| Lead | Intro paragraph | Söhne Buch 400 | 17–22px | 1.55 | Normal | 45–55ch | One short setup paragraph |
| Reading | Body | Söhne Buch 400 | 16px | 1.55 | Normal | 55–65ch | Explanation and evidence |
| Metadata | Eyebrow / state | Söhne Mono 400 | 11px | 1.4 | 0.12em | Short only | Index, state and provenance |
| Action | Button / action link | Söhne Kraftig 600 | 15px | Control-defined | Normal | Short only | Clear verbs, never prose |

- Use the role token, leading and measure together; do not choose size in isolation.
- One H1 per page. H2 opens a section. H3 names a contained idea. Never skip semantic levels to create a visual effect.
- Eyebrow to headline is 24–36px. Headline to lead is 24–40px. Paragraph spacing is one body line.
- Hierarchy comes from scale, measure and space. Never simulate hierarchy by making Signifier bold.

### Text selection on color

- Default selection uses translucent Signal Cyan and preserves the authored text color. It never changes display sizing or leading.
- On Signal Cyan backgrounds, selection inverts to translucent Ink with Paper text so the selected range stays unmistakable.
- Apply the production class `cw-surface-signal` to every content surface whose field is Signal Cyan. The class is semantic, not decorative.

## Action hierarchy

Codewave uses two semantic action tiers inside one authored silhouette: a 52px-high capsule with a 45px radius and a long hairline arrow. There is no leading dot or internal symbol. Quantum is expressed through state, not decorative machinery.

### Tier 1 — Resolved primary

- Use only for a consequential commitment that begins a relationship or materially changes the visitor's path.
- Approved examples: **Start a conversation**, **Book a quantum sprint**, **Begin transformation**.
- Show no more than one dominant primary in a section. Never repeat it across a card grid.
- Signal Cyan means the choice has resolved. Hover softly lifts the capsule and completes the arrow line.

### Tier 2 — Quiet secondary

- Use for a purposeful next step inside an existing journey.
- Approved examples: **View case study**, **Explore capability**, **Read the brief**, **Continue**.
- It may repeat across a page, but never cluster secondary actions into a wall of equal emphasis.
- The control stays transparent and outlined. Hover may resolve it to Ink while the long arrow opens to full length.

### Tertiary navigation

- Either tier may appear as a quiet underlined text action when its context already supplies enough visual emphasis.
- Do not use leading dots, clipped or notched corners, tunnels, rails, slits, alternate bright CTA colors or decorative quantum particles.
- Hover, keyboard focus and touch must reach the same final state. Reduced motion resolves immediately without losing hierarchy or meaning.

## Icon grammar

The custom **Quantum Glyph** family uses a 24 × 24 grid, 1.5 px strokes, one deliberate discontinuity and a small observation node. Glyphs describe state and relationship, not literal objects. Their motion is part of their meaning:

- possibility drifts;
- observation appears immediately;
- collapse converges;
- entanglement draws a relation;
- interference reveals locally.

Do not mix Quantum Glyphs with filled corporate icon packs in the same experience.

## Motion grammar

- 120 ms: acknowledgement.
- 240 ms: interface hover and focus.
- 620 ms: direct peer-card expansion or collapse; begin within 40 ms of hover or focus.
- 640 ms: observation and state change.
- 960 ms: narrative connection or collapse.
- 1450 ms: autonomous signature entrance or cinematic sequence; never direct hover response.
- 6 s: low-amplitude ambient editorial drift.

Motion should explain causality. Perpetual animation is reserved for ambient editorial fields; controls settle. Reduced-motion users receive an immediate final state without loss of information.

An observation aperture must always reveal a different semantic state. Never animate an empty ring over an unchanged image. The default field remains unresolved; the resolved artwork is visible only inside the aperture until the user disengages.

## Image prompt recipe

Use this order when commissioning or generating artwork:

1. **Argument:** the consequential choice or tension.
2. **Human anchor:** one recognisable action, gesture or expression.
3. **Quantum behavior:** Collapse, Entangle or Interfere.
4. **Impossible relation:** duplicated outcome, disproportionate connection or overlapping action.
5. **Print treatment:** high-contrast monochrome photography, flat duotone, raw halftone grain, hard cut-paper edges, slight registration error.
6. **Color recipe:** selected system palette only.
7. **Observation rule:** Signal Cyan appears only on the resolved meaning.
8. **Negative constraints:** no hardware, holograms, sci-fi glow, generic data visualizations, gradients, corporate stock-photo polish or embedded text.

## Voice

Codewave sounds like a sharp editor and a generous builder.

- Lead with the tension, not a capability list.
- Prefer precise verbs: choose, test, connect, shape, resolve, build.
- Make one surprising claim and support it with evidence.
- Use dry humor sparingly; never trivialize a client's risk.
- Avoid "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "seamless," "leverage" and unqualified AI superlatives.

## Approval and governance

Before shipping an asset, confirm:

- The idea is understandable in grayscale.
- Signal Cyan has one semantic job.
- Only one quantum behavior leads.
- The image is human-first and does not resemble a GPU or infrastructure company.
- Typography uses Signifier Regular and Söhne correctly.
- Hover, focus, tap and reduced-motion states convey the same meaning.
- Contrast, alternative text and reading order are documented.

The homepage remains outside this approval package until the full system is reviewed.
